# Dif app icon

The source image is `Dif-icon-master.png`. The macOS icon sizes in `Dif/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset` were resized from it with `sips`.

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for DIF. Final prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished production macOS application icon for DIF, a local text comparison app. Create one square 1024×1024 icon artwork. A deep saturated indigo rounded-square tile with genuine transparent pixels outside the rounded-square silhouette. Centered on the tile are TWO large, equally sized ivory document sheets side by side with small folded top-right corners, separated by a clear narrow gap. Each document has three bold, simple horizontal text strokes aligned row-for-row: the top and bottom strokes are muted warm gray and match, while the middle stroke on the left is vivid coral red and the middle stroke on the right is vivid mint green. In the gap, place one small but clear ivory bidirectional comparison arrow at the level of the colored middle strokes; it must read as comparing the two pages, not as a writing tool. Crisp geometric vector-like shapes, balanced symmetry, high contrast, subtle depth only, generous padding, legible at 16 px. No feather, no quill, no pen, no nib, no pencil, no letters, no words, no watermark, no scene outside the icon.
